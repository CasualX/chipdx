Chip's Challenge Bingo Help

Object of the game

Light five squares in a straight line: across, down, or corner to corner. The CHIP! square in the middle is free.

Solo play
  1. Press Generate board, then start playing Chip's Challenge.
  2. When you do or encounter what a square says, click it to turn it on.
  3. Complete any line to make BINGO light up.

A “streamer says” square counts when you say it. Nobody has to hear you.

Streamer + viewers
  1. Generate a board, then press Copy invite link.
  2. Share the link with your viewers before the run begins.
  3. Call out events as they happen so viewers can mark their cards.

Everyone gets the same squares in a different arrangement.

Wrong mark? Click the square again.

Reshuffle card changes your layout and clears your marks.

New board chooses a new set of challenges.
